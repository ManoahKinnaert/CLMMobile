CLMMobile

CLMMobile is a meeting timer utility for congregation meetings. It runs as a local web application on your device and helps you keep track of talk times during the meeting so the program stays on schedule. Furthermore, it can display the remaining time during a talk.

The app works on desktop and mobile, and can be installed on iOS and iPadOS through the iSH app. The app can then be controlled through the browser of any device on the same network. Please see installation instructions for more on this
